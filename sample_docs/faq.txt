Northwind Tech Office FAQ

The Pune office is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays and is closed on weekends.

Visitors must be registered at reception and must wear a visitor badge at all times.

The cafeteria serves lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM. Lunch is free for employees on office days.

Parking is available in Basement 2. Employees must register their vehicle with the admin team to get a parking sticker.
